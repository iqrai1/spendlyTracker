Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only the information necessary to provide the service:

  • Account information — your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data — basic information such as login timestamps to maintain session security.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data from your device beyond what you explicitly enter.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate Spendly and provide you with a personalised experience:

  • Displaying your expenses and spending summaries.
  • Authenticating your account and keeping your session secure.
  • Improving the reliability and performance of the service.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond what is listed here.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorised access, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure password hashing.

You can delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all your personal data and expense records are permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may use minimal third-party infrastructure (such as hosting providers) to run the service. These providers are contractually bound to process your data only as directed by us and in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you at any time. You can manage most of this directly from your account settings. For any data requests you cannot fulfil yourself, contact us and we will respond within a reasonable time.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the app. We take privacy seriously and will respond promptly.
